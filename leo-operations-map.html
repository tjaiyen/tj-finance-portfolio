Every domain, risk, 90-day action, and KPI already on the main dashboard, redrawn as one relational graph so the connections between them -- not just the tables -- are the thing you're looking at. No new data: every node below reads directly from the same exported marts as the narrative dashboard.

How to read this map

Center = the program. The 11 spokes are every operating domain that appears anywhere across the dashboard's marts -- 4 have both an owned risk-mitigation action and KPI tracking, 2 (Mission Assurance, Regulatory Affairs) have a risk and action but no KPI-framework row, and 5 are KPI-tracked only with no currently owned mitigation action. Click a domain to expand it; click any node for its full who/what/when/where/why/how detail and to highlight its direct relationships.
